Topic and learner

Topic shortlist

I have experience in [your field or skills] and I am often asked about [common questions people ask you]. Help me list five possible course topics, and for each one note who it would help, the problem it solves and how much I would need to teach it well.

Topic and learner

Learner conversations

I want to test the course idea [topic] by talking to [number] people who might take it. Write ten open, non-leading interview questions about their current situation, what they have already tried and what is getting in the way, plus a short, friendly message I can send to ask for a 20-minute chat.

Topic and learner

Learner and outcome

Here are notes from my conversations with potential learners: [paste notes]. Summarise the patterns you see, then write a one-paragraph description of my ideal learner and one clear, realistic outcome they can expect by the end of the course, without overpromising.
