Positioning

Niche finder

I offer [services] and have experience in [industries/projects]. Suggest 5 niche positions (who + problem + result), and rate each on demand, competition and fit with my experience.

Positioning

One-liner

Write 10 versions of 'I help [who] [achieve what] by [how]' for my freelance business. Make them specific and free of buzzwords.

Positioning

Portfolio case study

Turn this project into a short case study: client [type], problem [x], what I did [y], result [z]. Structure: challenge, approach, outcome, quote placeholder.
