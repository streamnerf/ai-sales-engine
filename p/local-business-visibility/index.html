Your profile and listings

Profile description

Write a clear business description of around 700 characters for my map and local listings. I run [type of business] in [town or neighbourhood], serving [main customers], and what makes us different is [two or three honest strengths]. Keep it warm and plain, mention the area naturally once or twice, and avoid superlatives.

Your profile and listings

Categories and services

I run [type of business] and offer [list of services or products]. Suggest one primary category and up to four secondary categories that describe my business accurately, then write a short services list with a one-sentence description for each, in the order customers are most likely to look for them.

Your profile and listings

Details consistency check

Create a checklist I can use to make sure my business details match everywhere they appear, including maps, directories, social profiles, my website and printed materials. My correct details are [name, address, phone, website, opening hours]. Present it as a table with columns for where it appears, what to check and a tick box.
