Sample prompts

Three of the 14, so you can see how specific they are. The PDF has the rest, plus a short introduction.

Before the appointment

What I want

I have a [type of appointment] with [doctor, dentist or other professional] on [date] about [main concern]. Help me write a short list of what I hope to come away with, sorted into must discuss, would like to discuss and can wait for another time.

Before the appointment

Symptom timeline

Help me turn these notes into a simple symptom diary and timeline: [rough notes on what happened and when]. Set it out as a table with columns for date, what I noticed, how long it lasted, what made it better or worse, and anything I tried.

Before the appointment

Medicines and allergies

Help me organise a clear, current list of medicines and allergies from these notes: [medicines, doses, how often taken, supplements, known allergies and reactions]. Present it as a tidy table I can print or show on my phone, and add a short list of details I should check with my pharmacist.
