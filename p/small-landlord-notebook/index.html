Letting the home

Honest rental listing

Write a clear, honest rental listing for my [property type] in [area]. Key details are [rooms, rent, bills included, available date, parking, outdoor space] and its honest drawbacks are [drawbacks]. Keep it warm and factual, and end with a short list of what is and is not included.

Letting the home

Viewing day checklist

Create a checklist for showing [property] to prospective tenants, covering what to prepare beforehand, what to point out during the viewing, which documents to have to hand and what to note down afterwards. Keep it to one page.

Fair applicant questions

Give me a list of fair, non-discriminatory questions to ask rental applicants about practical matters such as move-in date, length of stay, household size and references. Flag any common questions I should avoid because they could be unfair or discriminatory, and suggest neutral wording instead.
