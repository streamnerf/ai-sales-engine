Making a first complaint

First complaint letter

I bought [product or service] from [company] on [date] and the problem is [what went wrong]. Write a short, polite first complaint that sets out the facts in order, says what I would like them to do, and asks for a reply by [date].

Making a first complaint

Refund, repair or replacement

My [product] from [company], bought on [date] for [price], has [fault]. Draft a calm letter asking for [refund, repair or replacement], explaining why this is a fair outcome and listing the evidence I can provide, such as [receipts, photos, order number].

Faulty product claim

My [product] developed [fault] after [length of time] and it came with a [length] warranty from [manufacturer or retailer]. Write a clear warranty claim that includes the product details, when the fault appeared, what I have already tried, and a checklist of documents I should attach.
