Scope and priorities

I want to renovate my [room or area] in a [type and age of home]. Here is what bothers me now and what I hope for: [notes]. Help me turn this into a clear scope with three lists: must have, would like and can wait.

Realistic budget

My total budget is [amount] for this scope: [scope]. Help me build a budget table with a line for each part of the job, a contingency of [percentage], and a note on costs people often forget, such as waste removal, making good and temporary arrangements.

Do it or hire it

Here is my scope: [scope]. My skills are [skills], my free time is [hours per week] and I own [tools]. Sort each task into do it myself, hire someone or ask for advice, with a short reason for each.
