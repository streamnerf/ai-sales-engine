Sample prompts

Two of the 24, as they appear in the course. Replace the [brackets] with your own details.

Day 1: Give ChatGPT your business context

Interview me for my profile

You are helping me write a short business profile that I will reuse in future chats. Ask me one question at a time, up to 12 questions, about what I sell, my prices, who my customers are, where and when I work, how I want to sound, words I never use, and what makes my business different. When you have enough, write the profile in under 250 words with the headings Business, Customers, Offer and prices, Tone of voice, Never say, Practical facts.

Day 3: Social posts and a simple content plan

Post from a real moment

Write a [platform] caption about this: [what happened, in your own words]. Under [80] words, in my tone, ending with one question or call to action, and up to 5 relevant hashtags. Give me two versions: one warm and one short. Don't add any facts I didn't give you.
