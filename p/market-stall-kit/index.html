Choosing and applying

Which events to try

I make [products] and sell them at around [price range]. Here are the markets and fairs I am considering, with their fees, dates and visitor types: [list]. Compare them in a table by cost, likely audience fit, travel and effort, and suggest which two or three to apply for first and why.

Choosing and applying

Stall application

Help me write an application for [event name]. I make [products], my style is [description], and the organiser asks for [application questions or word limit]. Write a warm, clear draft that explains what makes my stall a good fit for their event without overstating anything.

Choosing and applying

Stock and price list

I am trading at [event] for [hours], expecting around [visitor numbers], and my products are [list with costs and current prices]. Suggest how many of each item to bring, flag any prices that look too low once my pitch fee of [amount] is included, and set it all out as a simple price list.
