Monday 19 October 2026

A calm plan for the week

The week looks full before it has even started, and everything seems to need doing at once.

I'd like a calm, realistic plan for my week. Here is what's coming up:

Fixed appointments: [meetings, appointments, school runs, with days and times]
Things I need to get done: [tasks, roughly how long each takes if you know]
Things I'd like to make time for: [a walk, a call with a friend, a hobby]
My energy: [e.g. sharpest in the mornings, tired after 4 pm]

Please:
1. Spread the tasks across Monday to Friday so no day has more than about three main things.
2. Put demanding work where my energy is best.
3. Leave one unscheduled hour each day for what comes up.
4. Tell me honestly if the week is too full, and suggest what could wait or be dropped.
5. End with the one thing that would make the week feel like a success if I only did that.

Keep it short and in plain words, as a simple day-by-day list.

What the AI might answer

Monday: Finish the quarterly report (morning). Dentist 14:00. Free hour 16:00.
Tuesday: Call the insurance company. Team meeting 10:00. Walk at lunch.
...
Honest note: Seven tasks plus four appointments is a lot. The garage clear-out could move to next week without anything going wrong.
If you only do one thing: Get the report in by Wednesday.
