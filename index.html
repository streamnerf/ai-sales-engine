About

A small shop in Sweden.

Carlsson & Co. is a small, independent shop run by me, Mathias Carlsson, from Sweden. I started it because I kept rewriting the same prompts for the same everyday jobs: a post to plan, a CV to tighten, an email that needed to go out today.

Each pack is something I'd want on my own desk: short, specific and written to be used, not admired. No big promises, just better starting points for real work.

If something's unclear or you have an idea for a pack, I'd genuinely like to hear it. You can reach me through Gumroad after any purchase.

Questions

What exactly do I get?

A PDF for each pack, with written prompts, a short introduction and notes on how to use them. You download it straight after purchase.

Which tools do they work with?

Any chat assistant, such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Free versions work; you just paste the prompt and fill in the brackets.

Will this make me money?

No one can promise that, and we won't. The prompts save you time and give you a better starting point. The results depend on your product, your audience and the work you put in.

Is the budgeting pack financial advice?

No. It helps you organise your own numbers and questions. For investment, tax or debt decisions, talk to a licensed professional.

Can I share the prompts?

Use them as much as you like in your own work and your clients' work. Please don't resell or redistribute the PDFs.
