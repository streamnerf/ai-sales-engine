About

A small library of working prompts.

Carlsson & Co. is a small, independent shop that makes prompt packs in English: short PDFs of written prompts for everyday work, from planning a week to answering a difficult email. It is run by me, Mathias Carlsson, from Sweden.

Why it exists

Most of what people ask an AI assistant is the same handful of jobs: a post to plan, a CV to tighten, a client to follow up, a trip to sort out. A vague request gets a vague answer. A good prompt says who it is for, what to include and what shape the answer should take, and that takes time to write. These packs are those prompts, written once and carefully, so you can start from something good instead of a blank box.

How the packs are made

To be plain about it: the packs are drafted with the help of AI assistants. Each one is then reviewed, structured and edited before it is published, with brackets marking where your own details go and the prompts arranged in the order you would use them.

The packs don't promise results. A prompt gives you a better starting point; what you do with it is still your work. The budgeting, fitness, journaling, therapist-admin and law-office packs help you organise, plan and write. They are not financial, medical, psychological or legal advice.

What you get

  • A PDF. Each pack is one PDF with 11 to 20 prompts, a short introduction and notes on how to use them. Sets and the Complete Library bundle several packs into one file.
  • One payment, through Gumroad. No subscription and no account on this site. Gumroad handles checkout and delivers the download straight away.
  • Works with the assistant you already use. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another chat assistant, free versions included. Paste the prompt, fill in the brackets, and go.
  • Yours to use. Use the prompts as often as you like in your own work and your clients' work. Please don't resell or share the PDFs.

There are 95 packs in the library right now, grouped into categories. Each product page shows the full list of prompts and a few real pages before you buy, and the free prompts are a good way to try the style first.
